Billy

A personal bill tracker for Android.

Billy keeps track of recurring bills: credit cards, electricity, gas, broadband, mobile recharges and medicine refills. It reminds its owner before things are due, and it updates bills automatically from bank and provider SMS and emails.

Billy is a private, personal app for a single user. It is not distributed on the Play Store, and it has no accounts, no server and no ads.
