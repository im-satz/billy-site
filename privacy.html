Privacy policy

Billy, a personal bill tracker for Android. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Billy is a personal app used by one person on their own phone. It has no backend server, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

What Billy stores

  • Details of the bills you track: billers, amounts, due dates, payments and reminders.
  • Short excerpts of the messages a bill was detected from. For cards, only the last four digits are kept.
  • Your settings.

All of this is stored only on your phone.

Google data Billy uses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly). Billy reads your email on your phone to find bills, statements and payment confirmations, including PDF statement attachments. It never sends, changes or deletes email. Messages are processed on the phone, and only the bill details described above are saved.
  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata). Billy saves its encrypted backups in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. Backups are encrypted with a password only you know.

Billy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

SMS

With your permission, Billy reads SMS on your phone to detect bills and payments. SMS never leave the phone, except as described in the next section.

Optional AI help

This feature is off by default. If you turn it on and add your own Anthropic API key, Billy can ask Anthropic's Claude to help read a message its own rules couldn't understand.

  • Billy sends only a short snippet it builds itself. The snippet contains only amounts, dates, provider names and generic bill words. Everything else is replaced with a blank marker, including names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, and account, card and reference numbers.
  • If Billy can't be sure a snippet is safe, nothing is sent.
  • Full emails, statements, attachments and passwords are never sent.
  • Every snippet sent is recorded in a log on your phone that you can review.

Sharing

Billy shares no data with anyone, apart from the optional, scrubbed AI snippets described above.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Billy deletes everything stored on the phone.
  • To delete backups, remove Billy's app data from Google Drive (Drive settings, then Manage apps).
  • To revoke Billy's access to your Google account, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
